# Case study sources

The case study uses Carlos Ocasio Milanes's 2024 industrial design senior-project
process book, supplied as `Carlos Ocasio.pdf`. An unchanged copy is served as
`public/case-study/MySpice-process-book.pdf`. Page numbers below are PDF page
numbers, including the cover.

The page summarizes interview themes without inventing quotations, sample sizes,
survey results, usability metrics or measured product outcomes. Design implications
are presented as directions derived from the findings. Historical health figures
are not repeated. The cost chart/table reproduces the original p. 9 figures, labeled
as historical 2024 research in USD, without adding an unsupported unit or treating
them as current prices. Differences use the original eating-out minus at-home values:
Illinois $20.00 / $3.85 / $16.15; Indiana $15.00 / $4.50 / $10.50;
Iowa $15.50 / $4.27 / $11.23; Kansas $12.00 / $3.58 / $8.42.
Current phone/paper support and satin silver direction
come from the user's subsequent design instructions.

## Visual research presentation

The six research symbols summarize qualitative themes, not relative frequency or
ranked survey results. The three audience groups derive from p. 13 without invented
demographics. The six-stage MySpice journey is an intended experience combining
the original meal journey (p. 16), app exploration (pp. 22–26), and the user's current
preferences, leftovers, grocery-list and ongoing-feedback brief. It is not a record
of observed usability outcomes.

`public/case-study/graphics/symbols.svg` contains custom line drawings created in
SVG for a precise, restrained editorial style. The interface uses native vectors,
an HTML bar chart/table, and selectable journey stages so graphics stay sharp,
readable and accessible at different screen sizes.

## Editorial image revision, 2026-10-20

At the user's request, the four supplied silver product images were remade using
the built-in image generation tool. The update explores softer studio lighting,
satin metal and finely ground/cracked spices, including a finer pepper texture.
The page applies a restrained saturation grade to the new product images.

The selected PNGs are in `public/case-study/editorial/`, with optimized transparent
WebP variants named `silver-front-editorial`, `silver-angle-editorial`,
`silver-right-editorial`, and `silver-left-editorial`. The page shows only the
front image at the end; the view selector has been removed. The other views remain
available as project assets. Prompts are recorded in `editorial-render-prompts.md`.
